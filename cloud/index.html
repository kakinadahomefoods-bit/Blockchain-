FOR YOUR VIVA

What the cryptography actually guarantees

Solidity can limit authorized writers and reject duplicates on the local EVM. A locally operated Ganache network is not independently decentralized; it can be reset by its operator. Browser-only hash chains provide tamper evidence but no external immutability. Facial recognition is not liveness verification and can make mistakes.
